What is Data Science

Turning Data Into Decisions

Data Science is the multi-disciplinary field that uses statistics, programming, and machine learning to extract insights and patterns from raw data — then transforms those insights into business decisions, products, and predictions.

It blends Mathematics, Computer Science & Domain Expertise to build everything from recommendation engines to self-driving cars and ChatGPT.

  • Collect and clean data from multiple sources
  • Analyze patterns using statistics & visualization
  • Build predictive models with ML & Deep Learning
  • Deploy AI solutions that drive real business value
